## The hour

### 0:00–0:05 · The split (hook)

- Open with the premise: *"Most agents can sell. A few can prompt. Almost none can build. In the next hour you'll leave able to do both."*
- The stakes, fast (2025 NAR data, same figures as the Big REveal deck):
  - 60% of 1.3M licensed agents sold **zero** homes last year
  - $58K average agent income on ~7 closings
  - 120K agents left the industry
- Reframe: the line isn't new vs. experienced. It's **equipped vs. unequipped** — and equipment is now a decision, not a budget.

### 0:05–0:15 · Strategist: where AI actually pays (and where it's hype)

- The three places AI moves money in this business **today**:
  1. **Speed to lead** — the first response wins; whoever owns the first sixty seconds owns the customer
  2. **Neighborhood authority** — research depth nobody expects from a solo agent
  3. **Reps** — practicing the hard conversations before they're real
- What's hype: AI that writes generic listing copy, "AI CRMs" that are autoresponders in a trench coat, anything that promises leads without a system behind it.
- Rule of the hour: **never let AI say a number you can't defend.** Everything you're about to see is grounded in real MLS data or a real fact sheet — no exceptions.

### 0:15–0:30 · Demo 1: Farm like you have a research department

- **Preview: the Stonoview Neighborhood Index** (live scroll-through).
- Story beats, in order:
  - 11 years of MLS history — 134 months, 449 sales — not a 12-month snapshot
  - The finding a snapshot would miss: the neighborhood's premium over its own island compressed from 32% → 26% in twelve months
  - The kitchen-table payoff: *anchor to 2025–26 closings, not the 22-sale 2024 peak; price right the first time; scarcity is the seller's real advantage*
- The teach: this took **an afternoon**, not a data-science degree. Method: pick the farm → export solds from your MLS → run the Neighborhood Index prompt (in the Vault) → publish and mail it.
- Land the point: *"When you hand a seller eleven years of their own street, you're not one of three agents interviewing. You're the one who did the homework."*

### 0:30–0:42 · Demo 2: The listing that answers its own phone

- **Preview: the AI listing assistant** — QR on the sign rider → a page for *that one listing* → an assistant grounded **only** in that listing's fact sheet.
- Do it live: audience member scans, asks a question, assistant answers from the facts, asks for name + cell before the deep stuff — and the SMS hits your phone **in the room**. (Phone on loud. Let them hear it.)
- Why it works — the three rules:
  1. One QR **per listing**, never one for the whole business — the per-listing page is the product
  2. The assistant never answers past the fact sheet — "I don't have that on file" beats a hallucinated school district
  3. The lead routes instantly — captured to your phone in under a minute, not digested into a Friday spreadsheet
- Frame: *"Capture the lead before the portal does."*

### 0:42–0:48 · Demo 3: T2Ks

> **[CONFIRM CONTENT]** — slot reserved. Same structure as demos 1–2: show it live, name the rule that makes it work, connect it to a dollar outcome.

### 0:48–0:55 · Builder: the one-week playbook

- Walk the Prompt Vault (they get all three):
  1. **The Neighborhood Index prompt** — farming authority from your own MLS export
  2. **The grounded listing assistant prompt** — the system prompt behind demo 2
  3. **The objection sparring partner** — ten rounds against the toughest version of your next appointment, scored
- Homework, day by day: Mon pick the farm · Tue export the data · Wed run the index · Thu pick one listing for a QR page · Fri practice one hard conversation ten times.
- Honesty beat: *"Everything I showed you, you can build yourself — that's why I'm handing you the prompts."*

### 0:55–1:00 · The close: build alone, or build in a room

- The pivot, verbatim honest: *"You now have the tools. The difference between having tools and having systems is the room you're in. The Agent Connection is a room where this is already running — the lunches, the mentorship, the systems, the people."*
- Commitment ladder on the final slide (never a dead end, lowest friction first):
  1. **Text/comment for the toolkit** — everyone gets this, no qualifier
  2. **Come to the next Big REveal lunch** — lunch is on us
  3. **Coffee / 15-minute call** — for the ones already leaning in
- Final slide = the QR → next-steps page (Door 2 of the funnel). Every scan is a captured, segmented lead.

## Rules of engagement (non-negotiable)

- Never claim a number you can't defend on real data. No income promises, no "100%," no invented conversion stats.
- Every demo is real or clearly labeled a replay. Never fake a confirmation the system didn't perform.
- Leads captured in the room get a same-hour response. The funnel's job isn't finished at capture — it's finished at a booked conversation.
- Teach generously. The prompts are the ad.
