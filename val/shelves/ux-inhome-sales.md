# In-Home Guided Consult (iPad, rep-facing)
Pull when: a rep runs a high-ticket close in front of the customer on one screen. Reference: 50Floor sales app.
- 3 phases in persistent top nav: 01 Discover / 02 Why Us / 03 Your [Product]; customer name pinned in header
- Discover: ~8 numbered TAP-ONLY questions — multi-select chips, labeled sliders, 0-10 importance sliders, image-card picks; split layout (dark question / light answer)
- Why Us: credibility deck — trust badges, reviews w/ NAMED local person, service-area map, competitor matrix, emotional close
- End: Thank You → single CTA ("View Job")
- Principle: rep never types during the meeting; every input is a tap
Never: a text field, a dropdown, or a scroll-to-continue during the meeting. Never an anonymous review where a named local one belongs.
Confirmed: 2026-08 — 50Floor sales app.
Done when: a rep finishes Discover in under 5 minutes and the keyboard never appears.
