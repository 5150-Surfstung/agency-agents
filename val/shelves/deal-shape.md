# Deal Shape
Pull when: pricing, packaging, scoping, retainers — "how do we charge for this."
- Front door is a paid diagnostic, never a free audit: it qualifies the client, scopes the build, and gets paid
- Build is fixed-fee off the diagnostic's findings — no fixed quote exists before the diagnostic is done
- Hardware and infrastructure at cost, client-owned; margin lives in the build and the retainer
- Retainer sells a recurring artifact the client can hand to a third party (report, audit log, review), not "support"
- Every phase ends in something the client owns and could take elsewhere
Never: quote the build before the diagnostic. Never free-audit to win the deal. Never sell a retainer with no artifact attached.

Done when: the client has paid before you have scoped, and every phase hands them an asset.
